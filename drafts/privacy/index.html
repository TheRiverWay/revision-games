Privacy

Last updated [date]. This page explains what information LevelWise keeps, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

The short version, for students

We keep your first name or nickname, the answers you type, and how you’re getting on in your game. We use that to pick questions that help you, and to show your parent and your tutor how you’re doing. We don’t show you adverts, we don’t sell your information, and nobody else can see it. If you want something deleted, ask your parent to email us.

Who we are

LevelWise (levelwise.co.uk) is run by [your full legal name], a sole trader based in England. For anything on this page, email hello@levelwise.co.uk. We are registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office: registration number [ICO number].

We don’t ask students for their email address, surname, date of birth, school or photo. Please don’t put a student’s full name in the names they choose for their game.

Our legal reasons

We use parent and student information to provide the service the parent has signed up for (a contract). We keep the service secure and fix problems because we have a legitimate interest in doing so. Payment records are kept because the law requires it. We share a student’s progress with their tutor only when the parent has agreed.

How questions are chosen

The game looks at which topics a student gets right and wrong and uses that to choose the next questions. That is the only automatic decision-making we do, and it only affects which maths questions appear.

What we never do

No adverts. No selling or renting information. No tracking or advertising cookies, and no analytics that follow anyone around the internet. Nothing in the game can be bought with real money.

Cookies and browser storage

We only store what’s needed to keep you signed in, in your browser’s storage. There are no advertising or tracking cookies, so there’s no cookie banner.

How long we keep it

We keep account and game information while the account is open. If you close your account, we delete it within [30] days. Payment records are kept for six years, because HMRC requires it. Students’ answers may be kept after that only with the name removed, so they can’t identify anyone, to help us improve the questions.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to how we use it. Parents can do this for their children. Email hello@levelwise.co.uk and we’ll reply within one month. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes

If we change this page in a way that matters, we’ll email parents before the change takes effect.
